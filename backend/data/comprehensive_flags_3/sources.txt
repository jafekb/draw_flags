Flag Database Sources
================================================================================

This comprehensive flag database was compiled from the following sources:

1. Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org)
   - National flags
   - Country subdivision flags (states, provinces, territories)
   - City and municipal flags
   - International organization flags
   - Historical flags

2. Flags of the World (FOTW) (https://www.crwflags.com/fotw/flags/)
   - Military flags
   - Naval ensigns
   - Political flags
   - Sports flags

All flag images are used under their respective licenses.
Please refer to the original sources for licensing information.
